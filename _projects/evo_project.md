It was around this time I decided I would work on exploratory projects every year in January, so in January of 2025 I 
began to investigate evolutionary algorithms as a potential research area for my PhD. My university (GVSU)
has a lot of great faculty working in the area, and [my brother](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=AcTEViAAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate)
did his PhD on evolutionary algorithms, so I thought the interest might be genetic.

I didn't end up liking the project too much, mostly because the biology didn't interest me and the evolutionary 
strategy for exploring a solution space seemed suboptimal to me due to the simulation required. It was still 
very cool and I'm glad that I did it, because it gave me a very valuable perspective on a method of problem solving 
which I can see being very useful in especially complicated problem spaces, like living on Earth. :)
